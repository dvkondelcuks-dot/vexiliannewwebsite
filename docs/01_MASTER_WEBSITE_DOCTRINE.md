# 01_MASTER_WEBSITE_DOCTRINE.md

## Project

Vexilian hospitality landing page for Latvian hospitality owners.

This document is the governing source of truth for all planning, copy, illustration, SEO, and design decisions. Any AI assistant, Cursor model, or developer working on this project must follow this document before making creative or structural decisions.

## Strategic Position

Vexilian is not presenting itself as a website agency.

Vexilian presents itself as a premium commercial operator / consultant for Latvian hospitality businesses that lose potential clients because enquiries, follow-ups, offers, content, and reservation paths are not commercially connected.

The website must sell clarity, control, and revenue visibility, not “digitalisation,” “infrastructure,” or “CRM setup.”

## Primary Audience

Latvian hospitality owners who run hands-on businesses and have relied too long on manual work, fragmented communication, Booking/Airbnb dependence, old content, inconsistent follow-up, or owner memory.

Primary ICP:

1. Guest houses
2. Event properties
3. Pirts / sauna complexes
4. Countryside stays
5. Mid-to-high class hospitality places with direct-booking potential

The owner should feel:

> This is exactly my situation. I have interest coming in, but I do not fully know where clients disappear, what should be followed up, or how to generate bookings in weaker months.

## Excluded Audience

This website should not speak to:

1. Restaurants
2. Cafes
3. Bars
4. Low-ticket hospitality
5. Generic Airbnb-only hobby hosts
6. Businesses with no serious booking or repeat-client potential
7. Businesses that only want a cheap website
8. Businesses that are not ready to improve commercial discipline

## Core Problem

Hospitality owners are not only losing clients because their website looks outdated.

They lose clients because:

1. Enquiries arrive from too many places.
2. Follow-up is inconsistent.
3. Off-season demand is weak or reactive.
4. Offers are not structured around seasons and guest intent.
5. Content does not guide people toward reservation action.
6. Owners do not know which channel produced which client.
7. Old guests are not reactivated properly.
8. Booking platforms bring visibility but do not build the owner’s own sales system.

## Core Promise

Vexilian helps hospitality owners find where potential clients disappear and builds a clearer path from interest to reservation.

Latvian formulation:

> Mēs noskaidrojam, kur jums pazūd klienti, un palīdzam sakārtot ceļu no pirmās intereses līdz rezervācijai.

## Main Offer Naming

Public-facing first step:

**Vexilian Klientu Noplūdes Audits**

Paid/commercial system name:

**Vexilian Rezervāciju Noplūdes Sistēma**

Alternative shorter naming:

1. Klientu Noplūdes Audits
2. Rezervāciju Noplūdes Sistēma
3. Viesu Ceļa Audits
4. Rezervāciju Kontroles Sistēma

Recommended usage:

Use “Klientu Noplūdes Audits” for the CTA because it is understandable and problem-led.

Use “Rezervāciju Noplūdes Sistēma” inside the methodology section to describe the larger commercial work.

Avoid using “Vexilian Sales Leakage System” on the Latvian homepage. It sounds foreign and more technical than the ICP needs.

## Main CTA

Primary CTA:

> Noskaidrojiet, kur jums pazūd klienti

Secondary CTA:

> Apskatīt, kā rodas rezervāciju noplūde

Avoid:

1. Sazināties
2. Uzzināt vairāk
3. Pieteikt konsultāciju
4. Sākt projektu
5. Saņemt piedāvājumu

Reason:

The CTA must feel like a diagnostic action, not a generic agency contact button.

## Conversion Type

The first conversion is a free audit.

Use the phrase:

> Bezmaksas klientu noplūdes audits

Do not call it a “free consultation” unless necessary. “Consultation” sounds generic. “Noplūdes audits” is sharper and more ownable.

## Post-Form Action

After form submission:

1. User sees a clear thank-you state.
2. User receives an automatic email confirming that Vexilian received the request.
3. The email states that a team member will contact them by WhatsApp or phone soon.
4. Vexilian receives an internal Slack notification.
5. Submission is stored with structured lead-scoring fields.
6. Lead is classified by fit and urgency.

## Required Lead Scoring Logic

The form must help quickly score whether the business is worth pursuing.

Score based on:

1. Property type
2. Average booking value
3. Monthly booking volume
4. Weak/off-season problem
5. Current enquiry channels
6. Website URL
7. Booking/Airbnb dependence
8. Whether they collect direct enquiries
9. Whether they follow up manually or systematically
10. Whether they have past guest data
11. Whether they want more direct bookings
12. Whether they are willing to improve content/offers
13. Urgency of problem

High-fit lead:

1. Guest house, event property, pirts/sauna complex, retreat, or countryside stay
2. Has real reservation value
3. Gets enquiries from multiple channels
4. Has weak months
5. Does not know which channels create reservations
6. Wants more direct or repeat bookings
7. Has some existing demand but poor control

Low-fit lead:

1. Wants only a cheap website
2. Has no meaningful booking value
3. Has no interest in follow-up or measurement
4. Has no direct booking potential
5. Operates only as a casual Airbnb listing with no ambition

## Pricing Strategy

Show pricing ranges, not exact fixed packages, unless a final commercial decision has been approved.

Reason:

The offer must feel serious, selective, and commercially evaluated.

Recommended public wording:

> Darba apjoms ir atkarīgs no pieprasījumu kanāliem, satura stāvokļa, sezonālās problēmas, rezervāciju procesa un tā, cik daudz jābūvē no nulles. Precīzs apjoms tiek noteikts pēc audita.

Internal draft range direction:

1. Audit: free for selected hospitality businesses
2. Commercial system build: from approximately €2,500 to €8,000+ depending on scope
3. Ongoing optimisation: from approximately €400 to €1,200+ per month depending on cadence and responsibility

Public pricing should remain softened:

1. “Audits: bez maksas atlasītiem hospitality uzņēmumiem”
2. “Sistēmas izveide: sākuma budžets pēc audita”
3. “Mēneša uzraudzība: pēc apjoma”

Only show exact numeric ranges publicly after Vexilian confirms the commercial threshold.

## Proof Strategy

Current proof is limited.

Therefore the site must not invent:

1. Case study revenue numbers
2. Percentage gains
3. Guaranteed booking increases
4. Fake client results
5. Fake hospitality outcomes

Allowed proof:

1. Founder commercial system thinking
2. Vexilian methodology
3. Process clarity
4. Certifications
5. Existing knowledge of commercial sales systems
6. Hospitality market research once sourced
7. Example diagnostic scenarios
8. Purgaiļi as a future/founding direction only if carefully framed

Purgaiļi rule:

Do not present Purgaiļi as completed proof. It may only be mentioned as a related hospitality direction, founder context, or internal case direction if payment/project is not yet active.

Correct phrasing:

> Vexilian tiek veidots ar praktisku skatījumu uz vietām kā viesu nami, pirts kompleksi un pasākumu īpašumi, tostarp mūsu pašu hospitality virzieniem.

Avoid:

1. Mēs jau palielinājām Purgaiļi rezervācijas.
2. Mūsu hospitality case study pierāda...
3. Purgaiļi pēc sistēmas ieviešanas sasniedza...

## Claims Rule

No percentage claims may be written unless backed by a current source.

If a page mentions “industry data,” “research,” “average uplift,” “direct booking growth,” or “retention gains,” the claim must have a source placeholder in the planning docs and must be verified before launch.

Use mechanism-led claims instead:

> Ja klients ir ienācis caur Booking, bet jūs viņu vairs nekad neuzrunājat tieši, jūs paliekat atkarīgi no platformas nākamās redzamības.

This is better than:

> CRM increases revenue by X%.

Allowed internal concepts:

CRM, tracking, attribution, automations, lead scoring, retention, campaign logic.

Public translation:

- CRM = viena skaidra vieta, kur redzams katrs pieprasījums, statuss un nākamais solis
- Attribution = redzams, no kura kanāla cilvēks atnāca un vai tas pārvērtās rezervācijā
- Automation = sistēma pati atgādina, kam jāraksta tālāk
- Lead scoring = ātri saprotams, kuri pieprasījumi ir vērtīgākie
- Retention = iepriekšējie viesi netiek aizmirsti pēc izrakstīšanās

## Tone

The tone must be:

1. Premium
2. Direct
3. Calm
4. Commercially specific
5. Slightly urgent
6. Owner-oriented
7. Latvian
8. Precise
9. Not agency-like
10. Not technical

It should feel like a sharp commercial diagnosis from someone who understands the owner’s real chaos.

## Homepage Emotional Balance

The page should feel slightly chaotic in the beginning, but controlled by the end.

Beginning:

> Pieprasījumi nāk no visurienes.

Middle:

> Šeit rodas noplūde.

End:

> Tagad ir redzams, kas notiek un ko darīt tālāk.

## Master Narrative

The homepage must follow this narrative arc:

1. Recognition: The owner sees their own messy incoming signals.
2. Diagnosis: The site names the actual leakage points.
3. Reframe: The problem is not only the website, Booking.com, or content. The problem is the missing commercial connection between interest and reservation.
4. System: Vexilian shows a clear method for capture, follow-up, visibility, and reactivation.
5. Trust: The site shows a serious, selective process without fake claims.
6. Action: The owner is invited to find where clients disappear.

## Final Strategic Rule

Every sentence, illustration, and section must answer one question:

> Does this help a Latvian hospitality owner understand where clients disappear and why Vexilian is the right operator to diagnose and fix that commercial leakage?

If not, delete it.
